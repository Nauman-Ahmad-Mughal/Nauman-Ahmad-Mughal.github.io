About me

A detail-oriented web developer who turns ideas and business requirements into clean, functional and visually engaging web solutions, with Laravel at the centre of everything I build.

Laravel is my main domain. I plan the database before I write a line of code, keep business logic where it belongs, and treat authentication, authorization and validation as part of the feature rather than something bolted on at the end.

I also design what people see. At Devnestic Solutions I create wireframes, user flows, prototypes and design systems, and I build interfaces in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS and Bootstrap, so a project can go from the first sketch to a working application without being handed off.

I work independently, ask questions early, and prefer a slow, correct build over a fast one that has to be rewritten.
